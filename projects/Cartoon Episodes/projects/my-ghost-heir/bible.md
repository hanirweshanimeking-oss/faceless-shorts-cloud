SHOW BIBLE — My Ghost Heir

SERIES
- Logline: An ordinary high school student inherits a cursed neo-gothic estate from an estranged dynasty, only to discover he has inherited an army of spectral nobility led by a cynical, aristocratic ghost ancestor who insists on grooming him to become the Lord of Spirits.
- Visual style: modern anime aesthetic, sharp cel-shaded anime feature look, Ufotable-grade ethereal cyan and violet spiritual lighting, atmospheric moonlit gothic shadows
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Default scene length: 15 seconds
- Style key: sheets/style-key.png

  - Voice: ancient aristocratic specter, theatrical, haughty, silky British-Japanese deep tenor
  - Personality & speech style: flamboyant, dramatic, obsessed with family legacy and royal etiquette, treats everyday modern objects with theatrical bewilderment
