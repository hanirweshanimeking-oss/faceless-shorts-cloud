### CHARACTERS IN THIS CHAPTER
1. **Aarav**: 16-year-old warrior youth, messy jet-black hair, copper skin, awakened golden solar left eye radiating ember wisps, red-orange Vedic dhoti with gold embroidery.
2. **Neel**: Sovereign of Night, 17-year-old anime prince, dark royal angavastram, jet-black hair with violet tips, wielding the glowing void-matter Trishul.
3. **Ananya**: 15-year-old temple priestess maiden, saffron-white silk sari, silver bindi, chanting Vedic mantras, wielding an amber lotus protective ward.
4. **Gilded Specters**: Ancient shadow chimera sentinels, insectoid dark constructs with golden runic shells.
5. **The Titan of Agastya (Teaser)**: Colossal clockwork Vedic automaton guardian entombed in the central spire.
