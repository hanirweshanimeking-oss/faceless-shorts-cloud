## 1. PREMISE & COSMIC ORIGIN

Before time shattered, **Siddhartha (The Golden Sovereign)** and his three brother sovereigns—**Hanirwesh (The Crimson Sovereign)**, **Vayuputra (The Azure Sovereign)**, and **Shunya (The Void Sovereign)**—stood at the center of the collapsing multiverse against the titanic Entity on the Colossal Black Throne. 

When the Entity unleashed the Seven Rings of Judgment to excise their existence from the cosmic karmic ledger, Siddhartha did the unthinkable: using absolute sovereign willpower, he ripped his immortal soul into **20 Memory Reclamation Keys**, hiding them deep within his existential marrow, and swore an oath:
> *"No matter what mud we wake up in... No matter what mortal names they brand us with... I will find you. And then we tear the sky down."*

## 2. THE MORTAL REBIRTH: IKE DRAGON

Siddhartha is reborn on Earth as **Ike Dragon**, the youngest son of the billionaire industrialist **Arjun Dragon** and **Meera Dragon**, and younger brother to **Ananya Dragon**, residing in a fortress-like estate in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad.

### The 10,000 IQ Anomaly
From birth, Ike possesses an extraordinary, transcendent intellect (**10,000 IQ**). Recognizing that raw power without memory is fragile, Ike spends his first eight years playing the role of a quiet, half-mute, introverted simpleton while secretly:
- Mapping the financial pipelines and offshore trust flowcharts of his family's multi-trillion-dollar conglomerate.
- Studying human greed, body language, and syndicate maneuvers.
- Memorizing every face, asset, and political puppet in Hyderabad.

## 3. THE HYDERABAD MASSACRE (THE CRUCIBLE MOMENT)

On a stormy monsoon night in Jubilee Hills:
1. **The Betrayal**: Security Chief **Raghav** turns his suppressed Glock on Arjun Dragon, revealing he was bought by Arjun's jealous brother **Vikram Dragon** in Delhi.
2. **The Jackal Enters**: **Devraj Rathore ("The Jackal")**, head of the ruthless national underworld syndicate, breaches the estate with heavy mercs to execute the entire bloodline.
3. **The Highway Execution**: On the Gachibowli Outer Ring Road flyover, an RPG flips the family's armored Maybach. Arjun and Meera are ruthlessly executed before Ike's eyes by mercenary lieutenant **Narasimha Naidu**.
4. **The Sludge Survival**: Eight-year-old Ike escapes into a six-inch drainage slit beneath the guardrail, submerged in freezing oil-slicked mud. As the killers ignite the wreck, Ike unclasps his father's platinum watch and makes an irreversible vow.
5. **The Sovereign Spark**: His golden sovereign aura leaks, creating an atmospheric gravity crater that shatters the rain before he vanishes into the underground sewer tunnels.

## 4. THE HYDERABAD ORPHANAGE & UNDERWORLD RISE

Presumed dead by the world, Ike ends up at a desolate, run-down government orphanage on the outskirts of Old City, Hyderabad. 

For ten years (Age 8 to 18):
- He lives under the radar as an obscure orphan.
- Uses his 10,000 IQ to build a covert underground intelligence and financial empire from cracked cybercafé monitors and burner phones.
- Takes up camouflage as a midnight delivery rider (Zomato/Courier) navigating every alley, gutter, and cartel safehouse in Hyderabad.
- Begins the sequential reclamation of his **20 Memory Keys**, unlocking ancient martial arts (Vajra Footwork, Sovereign Dragon Aura) and preparing to systematically annihilate Devraj Rathore, Vikram Dragon, and the global syndicate.
