## 1. THE HUMAN REALM: HYDERABAD & BEYOND

### 1.1 The Dragon Estate (Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad)
- **Geography**: A 14-acre fortified private sanctuary perched on the highest granite ridge of Jubilee Hills, overlooking the neon sprawl of Hyderabad and Hussain Sagar.
- **Architecture**: Italian Makrana marble, Venetian crystal chandeliers, 12-foot Burma teak doors, reinforced safe rooms, subterranean emergency escape shafts.
- **Atmosphere**: Once a haven of warm family meals and ancient library halls; now a scorched crime scene seized by Vikram Dragon's puppet corporations.

### 1.2 The Gachibowli Outer Ring Road Flyover (The Execution Mile)
- **Geography**: The 8-lane elevated expressway cutting through Gachibowli's high-tech corridor.
- **Significance**: The ambush site where an RPG flipped Arjun Dragon's Maybach. Beneath the guardrail sits the notorious 6-inch drainage culvert where 8-year-old Ike survived submerged in engine oil and mud, bearing witness to his parents' execution.
- **Atmospheric Anomaly**: The asphalt bears a permanent 50-meter circular dry crater where Ike's golden sovereign aura blasted rain away eight years ago.

### 1.3 The Old City Government Orphanage (Charminar Ward)
- **Geography**: A crumbling colonial-era brick compound hidden deep within the labyrinth of Old City, Hyderabad.
- **Atmosphere**: Peeling lime-wash walls, rain-stained tin roofs, flickering fluorescent tubes, rusty bunk beds.
- **Significance**: Ike's ten-year shelter. To the matrons and children, he is a quiet, sickly, introverted orphan who works delivery shifts at night. In reality, the orphanage attic holds Ike's encrypted server node, mapping out every hawala route and political bribe in South India.

### 1.4 Lad Bazaar & The Underworld Alleys
- **Geography**: The cobblestone alleys behind the historic Charminar, home to century-old pearl shops, attar merchants, and shadow hawala brokers.
- **Atmosphere**: Torrential monsoon rains flooding the stone lanes, flickering neon red signs, idling black SUVs, heavy paan smoke, and hidden blades.
- **Significance**: Turf of Narasimha Naidu and the Jackal's regional extortion rings.

### 1.5 Varma Tower 50th Floor Penthouse (Connaught Place, New Delhi)
- **Geography**: The pinnacle of New Delhi's financial power—a 50-story skyscraper of reinforced steel, titanium blast doors, and panoramic views of the capital.
- **Significance**: The regional throne of Devraj Rathore's syndicate, and the arena where Ike catches the Mach-7 meteor punch of the berserk Crimson Sovereign without spilling a drop of water.

## 2. THE COSMIC REALM: THE COLLAPSING MULTIVERSE

### 2.1 The Cosmic Graveyard
- **Geography**: The nexus of reality outside of space and time.
- **Atmosphere**: Jagged violet static fissures, pulverized nebulae, drifting star-dust, zero gravity, cold absolute silence.
- **The Colossal Black Throne**: A titanic monolith carved from dead universes where the Entity sits, judging and wiping civilizations from existence.

## 3. FACTIONS & POWER MATRIX

1. **The Four Sovereigns**:
   - **Siddhartha (The Golden Sovereign / Ike Dragon)**: Master of Universal Will, 10,000 IQ, 20 Memory Reclamation Keys.
   - **Hanirwesh (The Crimson Sovereign)**: Master of 4,000°C Bloodfire and Calamity Strength.
   - **Vayuputra (The Azure Sovereign)**: Master of Spatial Weaving and Celestial Speed.
   - **Shunya (The Void Sovereign)**: Master of Entropic Erasure and Absolute Silence.

2. **The Jackal Syndicate (Rathore Cartel)**:
   - Led by Devraj Rathore ("The Jackal"). Operates multinational corporate fronts, private military forces, and political assassination squads.

3. **Dragon Corporation (Compromised Loyalties)**:
   - Former sovereign industrial empire built by Arjun Dragon. Currently partitioned by traitor brother Vikram Dragon in Delhi and challenged by elder daughter Ananya Dragon.
