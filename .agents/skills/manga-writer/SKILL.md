## 2. Character Creation Protocol ("Like a Master Author")

Every character must NEVER be a generic archetype or cardboard trope. Every character is designed with 5 fundamental pillars of depth:

### Pillar 1: The Core Wound & Irreversible Conviction
- **The Crucible Moment**: What trauma, betrayal, or defining choice formed their worldview?
- **The Philosophy of Life**: What absolute truth do they believe about the world? (e.g., "The strong devour the weak," "Loyalty is the only currency that never devalues," "Kindness without power is suicide").
- **The Irreversible Oath**: The one line they will never cross—or the one thing they will sacrifice everything to protect.

### Pillar 2: The Mask vs. The Monster (Duality)
- **The External Persona (The Mask)**: How they present to society (playful goofball, cold businessman, meek outcast, elegant gentleman).
- **The True Monster Beneath**: What emerges when all pretenses shatter (unhinged bloodlust, icy calculation, feral survival instinct, divine wrath).
- **The Lie They Tell Themselves**: The false rationalization they cling to (their Want vs. their unconscious Need).

### Pillar 3: Distinct Vocal Signature & Cadence
- **Sentence Architecture**: Short clipped maxims vs. winding philosophical monologues vs. breathless interrogation.
- **Vocabulary & Idiom Tier**: Gritty street slang, corporate legalese, poetic archaic metaphors, or weaponized deadpan sarcasm.
- **Physical Dialogue Tics**: Speaking around an unlit cigarette, smiling while delivering a death threat, breaking eye contact during lies, whispering only when furious.

### Pillar 4: Threat Aura & Visual Anchor (Silhouette)
- **Focal Visual Anchor**: A distinct physical identifier (dual scars across the nose, mismatched glowing irises, tattoos winding up the neck, tailored suits stained in ink/blood).
- **Atmospheric Aura**: How the environment reacts to their entrance (heavy silence, shadows lengthening, dust freezing, distinctive visual colored trail—purple UI, crimson rage, golden precision).
- **Physicality & Posture**: Relaxed slouch of absolute confidence vs. coiled predator stance vs. rigid formal elegance.

### Pillar 5: Relational Friction & The Dynamic Counter-Weight
- **Why They Clash With Allies**: Even when their goals align, their methods, moral lines, and egos spark explosive conflict.
- **The Nemesis Mirror**: How their primary rival reflects their own fatal flaw back at them.

## 3. Master Dialogue Writing Rules

1. **Subtext Over Exposition**:
   - Characters never state their emotions directly. Their dialogue is an iceberg: 10% on the surface, 90% underwater.
   - Ban all "As you know" exposition. Backstory must be weaponized—surfacing only when a character is bleeding, cornered, or executing a betrayal.
2. **The "Thumb-Scroll" Dramatic Rhythm**:
   - Every line of dialogue must earn the next panel.
   - **The pregnant pause**: Use silence, eye-shifts, or environmental details between lines to build agonizing suspense.
   - End every scene/page on a revelation, an unanswered question, a sudden reversal, or an inescapable threat.
3. **Action-Integrated Speech**:
   - Dialogue happens during exertion, breath catches, joint locks, blade draws, and cigarette drags—never in static talking-head vacuums.
4. **Manga Balloon Architecture**:
   - **Spoken Balloons**: Punchy, razor-sharp, under 18 words per balloon.
   - **Internal Monologue (Thoughts)**: Rectangular narration boxes or square bracketed italics reflecting brutal honesty contrasting their outward spoken lies.
   - **Shout / Impact Balloons**: Spiky, distressed outlines with heavy hand-lettered bold typography.
   - **Whispers / Chilling Murmurs**: Small, thin font surrounded by expansive white space.

## 4. Standard Output Structure

When executing character creation and dialogue writing:

### Step 1: Character Dossiers (The 5 Pillars)
For each character:
- Full Name, Moniker / Epithet
- Pillar 1: Core Wound & Conviction
- Pillar 2: The Mask vs. The Monster
- Pillar 3: Vocal Signature (with 3 sample dialogue lines showcasing their cadence)
- Pillar 4: Visual Anchor & Silhouette
- Pillar 5: Relational Friction Matrix

### Step 2: Scene & Dialogue Script
For each scene:
- **Scene Objective & Stakes**: Who wants what, what is stopping them, what happens if they fail.
- **Dynamic Tension Curve**: Hook → Escalation → Reveal → Climax / Reversal.
- **Panel-by-Panel Breakdown**:
  - `[Panel X | Shot Type & Framing]` (e.g. Extreme Low Angle, Macro Eye Close-Up, Silhouette Gutter)
  - `[Visual Description]` Character posture, lighting, environmental atmosphere, kinetic motion lines
  - `[Dialogue]` Speaker, exact line, balloon styling (Standard, Whispered, Jagged Impact)
  - `[Internal Monologue]` If applicable
  - `[SFX]` Onomatopoeia with sound description (e.g., *뻐억 (BBO-EOK) - Heavy bone-crunching right hook*)
